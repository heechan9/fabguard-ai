# Exploratory note: Middle East PV heat and soiling

## Status

**Idea review only. Not a registered candidate, approved source, implementation
queue item, or connection commitment.**

This note preserves an early research question without mixing it into FabGuard's
public global candidate registry or implementation roadmap.

## Possible research question

Could high-temperature and desert-soiling conditions provide a useful opposite
stress extreme to the Finland cold/snow concept when testing PV data-quality
boundaries?

## Unverified leads

- Israel: search the official data.gov.il portal for an actual PV generation
  time series. The existence, accessibility, fields, license, units and timezone
  of a suitable series have not been confirmed.
- Saudi Arabia: inspect the claimed Jeddah residential-PV dataset associated
  with DOI 10.3390/en18112733 and its University of Southampton repository
  record. Dataset identity, access, reuse permission, schema, units and timezone
  all require direct confirmation.

## Explicit non-candidates

- UAE Shams Dubai installation-count statistics: installation counts are not PV
  generation time series.
- Qatar paid aggregate statistics: no suitable open generation time series has
  been established.
- Military-base energy data: excluded from review because public provenance,
  authorization and reusable licensing cannot be established.

## Revisit condition

Do not promote either lead to global_candidates.json or ROADMAP.md unless an
official source record, stable access path, explicit reuse terms, time-series
schema, units, timezone and an independently useful experiment are all verified.
France and the current first-scope PV sequence remain higher priority.
