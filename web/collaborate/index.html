From replay to a useful review

Pick one of these lanes and leave a short, asynchronous note. There is no meeting or patch requirement. The command-line replay uses the three roster cases above. Use the review receipt template for either exercise.

What can I review?

  1. Is the comparison question clear?
  2. Do both workflows use the same eligible records?
  3. Do both stay within the same review budget?
  4. Are missing records or replacements visible?
  5. Does the conclusion stay within the evidence?

What should I share?

  1. Scenario and fixture version.
  2. Exact expected result.
  3. Exact observed result.
  4. Evidence link or steps to reproduce.
  5. Why it matters and one useful next check.

For public feedback, use one finding per issue. Do not attach factory data, credentials, personal information, or confidential details. No sign-in is needed to use this page; GitHub may ask you to sign in if you choose to post.
